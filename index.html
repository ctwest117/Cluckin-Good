Popular Items

Our crowd favorites prove that chicken has had the spotlight long enough. Start with The Cluckin’ Good, a seriously beefy burger stacked with melty cheese, crisp toppings, and a sauce that refuses to be subtle. Prefer a little extra? The Moo-shroom Melt brings savory mushrooms to the party, while Steak Your Claim goes all-in for anyone whose appetite means business.

Menu

This is a burger place for people who believe a great bun deserves something bold inside it. Choose from classic, cheesy, smoky, and gloriously messy burgers; then branch out with our “Not Burgers” for when you want to rebel against your usual order. Round it all out with golden sides made for sharing—unless you’re the kind of person who wisely keeps the fries to yourself.

Where to find us

Find us where the grills are hot, the shakes are cold, and the beef gets the attention it deserves. Stop by hungry—leave happily outnumbered by fries. Only open on Sundays.
